    透析中的遊戲化之五附件：給 Shape Trail Test 研發團隊的信¶

    版本：v0.1　文件狀態：草稿，未寄出（2026-10-26 開立）　適用讀者：使用者、母計畫主持人

    上層文件：《透析中的遊戲化之五：有國際常模的第四款遊戲》第 6.4 節第 1 列；《之五附錄》第 9 節假設 A5

    摘要¶

    • 目的：問 STT 研發團隊能不能在平板上用 STT 的作法與常模，結果決定第 2 次迭代的 B 部分能不能叫 STT（假設 A5）
    • 收件人：香港中文大學心理學系 陳瑞燕教授（Agnes S. Chan），aschan@cuhk.edu.hk
    • 信箱依據：Ding、Chan 2024 論文的通訊作者信箱（1026 查證）；同一篇寫明 STT 由她的團隊在 1997 年研發
    • 信用英文寫；第 3 節是可直接複製的正文，第 4 節是中文對照，只給自己看
    • 寄出前要先填 6 個【】空格、請主持人過目並列副本（第 2 節）
    • 只寫好、沒有寄；寄出與回覆後更新第 5 節

    3. 信件正文（英文，直接複製）¶

    Subject: Request for permission to use the Shape Trail Test in a tablet activity for hemodialysis patients (National Cheng Kung University Hospital, Taiwan)

    Dear Professor Chan,

    My name is 【你的英文姓名】, an undergraduate student in the Department of Computer Science and Information Engineering at National Cheng Kung University (NCKU), Taiwan. Together with 【楊采綾的英文姓名】, a master's student in the same department, I am developing a tablet system that patients use during hemodialysis at National Cheng Kung University Hospital. The system is part of the research project "【計畫英文名稱】", led by Dr. 【劉冠宏醫師的英文姓名】, who is copied on this email.

    We are planning a short connect-the-dots activity that patients can do on the tablet during treatment, and we would like to base it on the Shape Trail Test developed by your team. Many of our patients are older adults who are not familiar with the English alphabet, so the STT's use of circles and squares instead of letters makes it much more suitable for them than Part B of the original Trail Making Test. We learned about the test from Zhao et al. (PLoS One, 2013) and from your recent paper with Dr. Ding (J Prev Alzheimers Dis, 2024).

    Before going further, we would like to ask for your permission and advice. This is how we plan to use it:

    • Part A (numbers 1 to 25 in circles) and Part B (numbers 1 to 25, each shown in both a circle and a square, connected in order while alternating between circles and squares), on a 10-inch tablet in landscape orientation.
    • Patients tap each item in sequence instead of drawing lines; a straight line appears automatically after each correct tap. Errors are recorded but not signaled, and no examiner is present.
    • To avoid stress and blood-pressure changes during dialysis, the instruction is "at your own pace" rather than "as quickly as possible", and no timer is shown. Completion time is recorded in the background.
    • A standard version with fixed layouts (designed by us, with matched difficulty) is given once a month, between the 30th and 90th minute of treatment. On other days patients may play a casual version with random layouts.
    • Results are shown only to nursing staff, mainly as a comparison with the patient's own previous results. We would also like to show a percentile against published norms, clearly labeled as reference only and not a diagnosis, together with the source of the norms and the testing conditions (during dialysis, on a tablet).
    • The use is academic and non-commercial.

    Our questions are:

    1. May we use the STT format in this way? If so, are there any conditions, such as a citation, an acknowledgement, a fee or a written agreement?
    2. Given the modifications above, may we call the activity the Shape Trail Test, or would you prefer wording such as "adapted from the Shape Trail Test"?
    3. Could you share the original test forms, standard instructions, practice items and scoring rules, so that our version stays as close to the original as possible? If you would rather we did not reproduce the original layouts, we will keep using our own.
    4. Which normative data would you recommend for older Chinese-speaking adults, stratified by age and education, and may we display percentiles based on them (for example, Zhao et al. 2013 or norms from your team)? Are you aware of any norms collected in Taiwan?
    5. Has your team developed or validated a digital or tablet version, and do you know how tapping instead of drawing affects the scores?

    If it is of interest to your team, we would be glad to share what we learn, such as completion rates and how tablet results compare with the paper version in this population, subject to ethics approval at our hospital.

    Thank you very much for your time, and for making this test available to the research community. I would be happy to provide more details or arrange a video call at your convenience.

    Kind regards,

    【你的英文姓名】

    Department of Computer Science and Information Engineering, National Cheng Kung University, Tainan, Taiwan

    【學校信箱】

    4. 中文對照（只給自己看，不寄）¶

    段落 意思
    主旨 請求在給透析病人的平板活動中使用 STT（成大醫院）
    第 1 段 自我介紹：成大資工大三、與碩二同學開發成大醫院透析中病人用的平板系統，屬於某計畫，主持人在副本
    第 2 段 想做一個連點小活動並以 STT 為基礎；很多長輩不熟英文字母，用圓與方比原版 TMT-B 合適；從 Zhao 2013 與 Ding、Chan 2024 得知這個測驗
    第 3 段 先徵求同意與建議；怎麼用：A、B 兩部分，10 吋橫放；用點的不用畫、點錯不提示、沒有施測者；說「照自己的速度」不說「越快越好」、不顯示計時；每月一次固定版面的標準版（透析第 30～90 分鐘），其他時候隨機版面；結果只給護理人員，主要跟自己比，也想顯示常模百分位並標明僅供參考與施測條件；學術、非營利
    問題 1 能不能這樣用；有沒有條件（引用、致謝、費用、書面協議）
    問題 2 改成這樣還能叫 STT 嗎，還是要寫「改編自 STT」
    問題 3 能不能提供原版題本、標準指導語、練習題與計分方式；不希望我們重製原版版面就繼續用自製的
    問題 4 華人長者依年齡與教育分層的常模推薦哪份、能不能拿來顯示百分位；有沒有台灣的常模
    問題 5 有沒有做過或驗證過數位、平板版本；用點的對分數有什麼影響
    倒數第 3 段 （可刪）若對方有興趣，願意在倫理審查通過後分享完成率、平板與紙本的比較
    結尾 感謝；可以補資料或約視訊

    刻意沒寫進信裡的：

    • 病人人數、醫院床數：還沒定，寫了就是承諾
    • 「已通過倫理審查」：這款遊戲目前沒有送審
    • FHN 的 300 秒切點與 FR-R 法規細節：對方用不到，信越短越容易回
